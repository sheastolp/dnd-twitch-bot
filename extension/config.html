Setup

  1. Connect GuildScribe to your channel at guildscribe.tavernworks.dev → Raise the Guild Banner.
  2. Activate this extension as a Panel (under your stream) and/or a Video Overlay (on the player).
  3. That's it — there's nothing else to set here. Which features show follows your GuildScribe dashboard switches.

Viewers see their own hero, purse and party once they press Share my Twitch name in the extension.
